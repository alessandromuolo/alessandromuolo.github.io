About Me

I am an Economist and PhD Candidate in Regional Science and Economic Geography at the Gran Sasso Science Institute (GSSI), Italy.

My research focuses on urban and regional economics, combining spatial analysis and quantitative methods to investigate urban development, regional disparities and the evolution of city systems.

For academic enquiries, please contact me at alessandro.muolo@gssi.it .

Research

My research interests lie at the intersection of urban economics, regional science and economic geography, with a particular focus on the spatial organisation and long-run evolution of urban systems.

Urban Systems and Zipf's Law

City-size distributions, urban hierarchies and their evolution across space and time.

Polycentricity and Metropolitan Structure

The spatial organisation of economic activities, urban centres and metropolitan development.

Regional Development and Spatial Economics

Spatial inequalities, infrastructure, regional economic dynamics and urban growth.
